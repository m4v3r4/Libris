# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Libris aims to be a welcoming, constructive, and respectful free-software project. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help keep discussions useful and safe, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or role.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that helps the project include:

- being respectful when disagreeing,
- giving technical criticism without attacking people,
- assuming good faith while still being clear about problems,
- accepting corrections and making room for less-experienced contributors,
- keeping issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project,
- respecting privacy and avoiding publication of personal or sensitive data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language,
- personal insults or sustained hostile behavior,
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention,
- deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated off-topic behavior,
- publishing another person's private information without permission,
- encouraging others to engage in abusive behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces used to represent or maintain Libris.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

If a conduct concern involves sensitive information, avoid posting those details publicly. Contact the maintainer through an available private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Enforcement decisions should prioritize the safety of participants and the health of the project.
